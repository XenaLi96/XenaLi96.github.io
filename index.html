COMPUTER SCIENCE · UC IRVINE

Xi Li.

Ph.D. researcher in computational pathology
and spatial genomics.

I build machine-learning methods that connect what tissue looks like with what its cells are doing—from histology-to-gene prediction to spatial modeling and reliable biomedical AI.

I am pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science at the University of California, Irvine (2023–present). Previously, I received an M.S. in Computer Science and Technology from Tsinghua University (2019–2022) and a B.S. in Information Security from the University of Science and Technology of China (2015–2019).
